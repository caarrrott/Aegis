# Travel and reimbursement policy

## Hotel and lodging limits

Under the v2 travel policy, employees travelling on company business may expense hotel lodging up to $180 per night before taxes, and receipts must be submitted within 14 days of returning from the trip.

## Meal allowance

Under the v2 travel policy, daily meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $45 per day.
