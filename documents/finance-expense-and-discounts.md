# Expense and Discount Policy

## Submitting a Reimbursement

Employees must submit expense reimbursements within 30 days of the transaction date with itemized digital receipts attached in the finance portal. Approved reimbursements are paid out on the next biweekly payroll cycle.

## Travel Expense Limits

Standard economy airfare must be booked at least 14 days in advance, and hotel lodging is capped at $275 per night before taxes unless pre-approved by Finance. Daily meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day.

## Customer Discount Approvals

Account executives may offer up to a 10 percent discount on annual contracts without special sign-off. Customer discounts between 10 percent and 25 percent require approval from the Finance Controller, and any discount above 25 percent requires CFO sign-off.
