# Customer Refund Shipping and Warranty Policy

## Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of delivery provided the item is returned in its original packaging with proof of purchase. Refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 5 business days of inspection.

## Shipping Options

Standard domestic shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over $50. Express two-day shipping is available for a flat fee of $15 at checkout.

## Warranty Coverage

All hardware products include a two-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover accidental physical damage, unauthorized modifications, or liquid damage.
