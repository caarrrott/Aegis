# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave

Under the previous policy, full-time employees received 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, and unused leave could not be carried over into the next year.

## Parental Leave

Under the previous policy, eligible employees received 8 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child.
