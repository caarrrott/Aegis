# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year in addition to public holidays. Employees may carry over up to 5 unused annual leave days into the first quarter of the following year.

## Parental Leave

All full-time employees are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave within the first 12 months of a child's birth or adoption.
