# Travel and reimbursement policy

## Hotel and lodging limits

Employees travelling on company business may expense standard hotel lodging up to $275 per night before taxes, and itemized digital receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the transaction date.

## Meal allowance

Daily meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day upon submission of itemized receipts in the finance portal.
